A newspaper front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026, drawn from
scratch in pure Python (standard library only) by `main.py`.

Lead story: the Gulf war widens — Iran says it hit US bases in Bahrain
and tankers keep burning in the Strait of Hormuz. Also inside: COP31
collapses in Antalya, the three objects that hit Poland were Russian
Kh-101 missiles, 24 body bags pulled from a burnt candy factory in
Cikande, at least 24 dead as a funeral boat capsizes in Nigeria, the
Kryvyi Rih toll rises to 73 injured, the Sparta II still burning in the
Gulf of Finland, and Peru counting the cost of the Junin quake — plus
Bitcoin's rebound and the Democrats' US House majority in the strip.

Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`; it writes
`out/entry.png` (1000x1000, deterministic: two runs are byte-identical).
